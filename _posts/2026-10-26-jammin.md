For this homework, I made an Arduino program that plays a song on the buzzer when a switch is on. When I turn the switch off the music stops.

I picked the Game of Thrones. I found the notes online at https://github.com/robsoncouto/arduino-songs/blob/master/gameofthrones/gameofthrones.ino

To make it play, I turned the song into a list of notes and how long each one lasts. The program plays them one by one with the buzzer. I put this in loop() so the song starts over again and again as long as the switch stays on. I connected the switch to the board with alligator clips and I set it up in setup() so the board could read it correct.

**Tip for past me:** Check the switch after every note not just at the start of the song. If you only check once, the music keeps going until the whole song ends. Checking between notes lets it stop almost right away.

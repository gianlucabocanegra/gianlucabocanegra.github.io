For this homework, I made a plushy with 3 LEDs that light up depending on how the slide switch and a button are in the plushy. I connected the LEDs, the battery, switch and button using conductive thread. Before starting sewing, I wanted to make sure of something, so I tested the circuit using alligator clips to make sure everything was working fine, and I sketched out the design as a paper prototype first. It was a really interesting homework that required a lot of patience.

**Tip for past me:** To test the circuit with alligator clips before I start sewing. It’s so much easier to fix a mistake when you can just unclip and move things around, instead of seeing something is wrong after it’s already stitched into the fabric. Great lesson learned for me.

For this homework, I wrote an Arduino program for the LilyPad USB Plus that cycles through 4 LEDs using conditionals. Every few seconds, a different LED turns on based on how much time has passed, and it keeps cycling through the pattern. 

Here is a photo of it working:

![Blinky LEDs](/assets/img/blinky-photo.jpg)

**Tip for past me:** Keep a separate variable to track which LED is currently on, instead of trying to figure it out every single time the loop runs. It makes the conditionals way easier to write.

For this homework, I traced the sewing sampler worksheet we collected in class onto a muslin fabric, and I had the chance to do 9 different stitches: running stitch, whip stitch, back stitch, cross stitch, blanket stitch, running stitch with small spaces, satin stitch. I chose 2 myself: split stitch and double cross stitch.

And here is the back:

![Back of sewing](/assets/img/sewing-back.jpg)

**Tip for past me:** Learn all 9 stitches by heart before starting the sewing homework. I spent around 6 hours to complete the homework, and I was pausing and watching Youtube videos a lot, which slowed everything down. If I had memorized the steps for each stitch first, I would have taken less time.

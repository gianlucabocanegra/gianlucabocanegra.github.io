For this homework, I made an Arduino program that figures out if a ghost is nearby. It uses a light sensor and a temperature sensor. When it is dark and cold at the same time, the two red LEDs and the red light on the board turn on. If it is not dark and cold enough all the lights stay off.

Here is a photo of my kit with the lights on:

![Ghost detector LEDs](/assets/img/ghost-detector.jpg)

**Tip for past me:** Change your dark and cold numbers while you test. Your room is probably not dark or cold enough to turn the lights on so I made the limits easier to reach first, checked that the lights worked, and then set the final numbers. Interesting homework, it took me a while to figure it out but the final product is working.
